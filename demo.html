Inside the prototype

Real places. Real risks.

Each scenario is built around what warehouse teams actually meet on shift: suspicious emails at workstations, shared scanners and terminals, and the everyday habits that keep systems safe.

  • Sign in as a traineeSecure log-in with demo accounts, so progress and results are tied to a person.
  • Look around in 360°A full warehouse scene. Drag with a mouse, or move your phone to explore.
  • Investigate six hotspotsGlowing markers are scattered at different heights and angles; click each one to reveal a risk.
  • Read the scenarioEvery hotspot explains what makes the situation dangerous in plain language.
  • Answer a short questionA quick check on each hotspot turns spotting a risk into knowing how to respond.
  • Get your scoreFinish all six to unlock your results, then restart to improve.
