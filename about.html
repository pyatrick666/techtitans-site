About Tech Titans

A small team making cyber security feel practical.

We build interactive training that puts warehouse teams inside the situations they actually face, so good security habits come naturally.

Mission statement
To make cyber security clear, practical and memorable for the people who keep warehouses moving.
What we stand for

Training people actually remember

Real places

We train people in a warehouse they recognise, not in abstract slides. Familiar surroundings make lessons stick.

Short and active

Five-minute modules fit into a shift. Learners click, explore and answer, rather than sit and listen.

Clear for everyone

Plain language, no jargon. Every member of the team, not just IT, can spot a risk and know what to do next.
